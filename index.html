Room 2

Artist's statement

I'm Otiniel, a designer and front-end developer in Odivelas, just outside Lisbon. I make landing pages and websites for brands that would rather be remembered than blend in.

My process is simple: find the one idea a page should leave behind, give it its own type, colour and words, then build it by hand so it loads fast and works on every phone. When a page needs to move, I use GSAP and Three.js. When it doesn't, I leave it still.

  1. 01Brief

    A short message or call: what you offer, who it is for, and what a visitor should remember.

  2. 02Concept

    One idea and its art direction, shown to you before a single line of code.

  3. 03Build

    Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, tested in real browsers and on phones.

  4. 04Launch

    Published, fast, and handed over so it is yours to keep.
