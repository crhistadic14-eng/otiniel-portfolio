公園

Parks for cities that forgot how to breathe.

We are a small studio in Tokyo and Lisbon. We design parks the way gardeners do: slowly, by watching where people walk and where the shade falls in August.

四つの公園

Four parks
2022 to 2024

一

Hinoki Walk 檜の道

Tokyo · 3.1 hectares · 1,240 trees

A former freight yard turned into a cypress walk. We kept the rails and let moss grow between them.

二

Fog Garden 霧の庭

Sapporo · 1.4 hectares · 420 trees

A small garden that waters itself from the morning fog, using nets borrowed from fishing boats.

三

Tejo Banks テージョの岸

Lisbon · 5.6 hectares · 2,150 trees

Five kilometres of riverbank given back to the city. Cork oaks, stone pines and benches that face the water.

四

Kawa Meadows 川の原

Osaka · 7.8 hectares · 3,800 trees

Meadows that flood on purpose. When the river rises, the park takes the water so the streets do not.

仕事の仕方

How we
work

  1. 一

    Plant first, pave later.

    We draw the trees before the paths, then wait a season to see where the grass wears thin.

  2. 二

    Shade is infrastructure.

    Every park we build reaches at least 40% shade by its tenth summer.

  3. 三

    Benches face the trees.

    Not the road, not the screen on the building opposite. People stay longer when something alive is in front of them.

  4. 四

    The edges grow wild.

    The middle can be tidy. The edges belong to tall grass, insects and the occasional fox.
