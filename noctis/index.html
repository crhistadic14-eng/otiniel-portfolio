01 / The idea

Most night perfumes smell like the club. We wanted the walk home: cold air, somebody else’s cigarette, and the rose that was still pinned to your coat.

02 / The bottle

Heavy
on purpose.

We wanted it to sit in the hand like a paperweight, and on a nightstand like an object rather than a product. 212 grams of glass, brass and paper.

  1. 01

    Cap

    Black anodised aluminium. It closes with a soft, heavy click.

  2. 02

    Collar

    Brass, crimped by hand onto the neck.

  3. 03

    Label

    Letterpress on cotton paper. Each one is numbered in pencil.

  4. 04

    Base

    14 mm of polished glass, so the liquid seems to float above the table.
